Freelance layihə · Project Management, Technical Writing, Report Writing, Project Scheduling, Risk Management, Data Analysis

I’m looking for a detail-oriented professional who can turn a live construction/engineering project brief I will supply into a full suite of management reports. The focus is on demonstrating best-practice planning techniques rather than writing generic theory, so each document must speak directly to the project’s scope, schedule, cost and stakeholder context. To be clear, I need four standalone reports: • Quality Management Report – set out a QA/QC plan aligned with typical site activities, inspection and test plans, non-conformance procedures and continuous-improvement loops. • Sustainability Report – address material selection, waste minimisation, energy/water efficiency and carbon considerations, referencing current green-building or infrastructure guidelines where relevant. • Stakeholder Management Report – map key internal and external stakeholders, outline engagement strategies and communication matrices that will keep the project team, clients, regulators and local community on side. • Earned Value Management Report – present a baseline time–cost model
