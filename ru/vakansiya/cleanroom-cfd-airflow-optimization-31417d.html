CFD Simulation and Airflow Optimization for Cleanroom 1A and 2A Computational Fluid Dynamics Study • Cleanroom 1A (3,450 m²) • Cleanroom 2A (1,000 m²) TYPE TOTAL AREA DURATION BUDGET 1. Project Overview We require a detailed Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) study for two cleanrooms only: Cleanroom 1A and Cleanroom 2A. No other areas are in scope. The goal is to validate and optimize temperature uniformity, humidity control, airflow distribution, and return air performance before final design freeze and commissioning. The system uses a combination of AHU supply air, FFU array, return air chase, and dry coils. The CFD must model the interaction between all four, not each in isolation. 2. Scope of Work Scope is strictly limited to the two cleanrooms below. Out of scope: all other cleanrooms, corridors, sub-fab, and mechanical rooms, unless they directly affect return air or plenum pressure for 1A / 2A.
