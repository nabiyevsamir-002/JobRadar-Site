Terms of use

These terms cover JobRadar AZ — the Telegram bot @JobRadarAzBot, jobradaraz.com and panel.jobradaraz.com. By using the bot or the site you agree to them. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

The service

JobRadar AZ collects vacancies from public job sites and Telegram groups and sends you the ones that fit. We are not an employer or a recruitment agency: an application goes straight to whoever posted the job, and they decide who is hired.

Job posts

  • A post's text belongs to its source. We try to check posts, but we do not guarantee their accuracy, the pay, or that a job is still open.
  • Never pay in advance to get a job, and never send card details, a photo of your ID or codes. If someone asks for that, it is most likely a scam: tell us in the bot with /feedback.
  • If a post contains your personal data and you want it removed, write to us.

Free and Pro

The main service is free. The search pass adds features (jobs as soon as they are found, up to 30 a day, the AI helper and so on). It is bought in the bot with Telegram Stars (current prices: /pro in the bot): the price and the days are shown before you pay, it does not renew by itself and closes when its days end. A pass opens only after Telegram confirms the payment. A lifetime Pro bought earlier stays valid. For any question about a payment, write /paysupport in the bot or to the contact address.

Rules

  • Do not use the bot for spam, abuse, pretending to be someone else, or disrupting the service. We may restrict accounts that break these rules without warning.
  • Do not scrape the site or resell the service under your own name.

Data

What we keep and how to delete it is in the privacy policy. In the bot, /stop deletes your profile and history.

Liability

The service is provided as it is: there may be breaks, delays and errors. We are not responsible for what the people who post jobs do.
