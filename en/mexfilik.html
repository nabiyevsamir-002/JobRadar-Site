Privacy policy

This page explains in plain words what data JobRadar AZ — the Telegram bot @JobRadarAzBot, jobradaraz.com and panel.jobradaraz.com — keeps, why, and how to delete it. Last updated: 22 October 2026.

What the bot keeps

  • Telegram data: chat id, your name, @username, Telegram language;
  • Job wishes: the fields and keywords you chose, city / district, minimum salary and, if you want, your gender (to filter posts meant for one gender only);
  • CV — if you send one: the file stays on Telegram, we keep a link to it;
  • Activity: the jobs we sent you, saved jobs, applications, reminders, "not relevant" marks, your last visit and how often you used the buttons;
  • Your messages: what you write to the bot and support chats (the support assistant hides card numbers, passwords and codes automatically);
  • Pro: the payment status only. The bot does not keep receipts — they are forwarded to the admin for checking.

Why

To find and send you suitable vacancies, improve the search and answer your questions. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising; only the JobRadar AZ admin can see it.

Where it is kept and who processes it

  • on the project's computers in Azerbaijan;
  • in a Supabase database (Germany, EU) — for backup and sync;
  • in a private GitHub backup (USA);
  • Telegram — messages travel through it;
  • AI services: Groq, Google Gemini and NVIDIA to understand job posts and your keywords; Anthropic Claude for the support assistant (your support messages, with sensitive data hidden). Your name and CV are not sent to them.

On the website

jobradaraz.com has no accounts, ad trackers or analytics — only one cookie to remember the light / dark theme. Posts are collected from public sources; if a post shows your personal number and you want it removed, write to us.

Web profile (panel.jobradaraz.com)

If you create a profile: name, e-mail, phone, photo, CV file and what you write in it. If you sign in with Google or Telegram we receive your name and e-mail. When you apply through the panel, your name, e-mail, phone, message and CV go to that employer.

Deletion

In the bot, /stop → "Yes, delete": your profile, CV link, saved jobs, applications, messages, support chats and statistics are deleted at once. Technical records (logs, monitoring) clear themselves within a few days; old backups may keep data for a while — to have them removed too, e-mail us. A web profile is deleted with "Delete account" on the profile page.

Employer data

An employer creates an account on the website with an e-mail and a password (the password is stored encrypted). When a vacancy and a company profile are created, we collect:

  • Public (shown on the website / in the post): company name, description, logo, website, field, city / address, the vacancy details and the public way to apply the employer gives;
  • Private (for moderation only, never published): contact name and phone / e-mail.

Every vacancy is checked by the admin before it is published. Vacancy statistics (views, clicks, number of notifications) are shown only in total — no candidate's personal data is revealed. When a candidate applies to the given address, their data goes to that employer / external website, i.e. to whoever receives the application. We do not sell this data. The history of closed vacancies is kept for audit; it can be deleted on request.
