How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in

    With Google, Telegram or e-mail — one minute. A company name is enough; add a logo and details later.

  2. 2

    Write the vacancy

    Title, city, work mode, salary and requirements. The AI helper drafts the text and the preview shows how it will look on the site.

  3. 3

    We review it

    The JobRadar team usually reviews it within 24 hours: approves it, asks for an edit or rejects it with a reason. We tell you by e-mail or Telegram.

  4. 4

    Candidates apply

    An approved vacancy is published on the site and sent to matching job seekers in Telegram. Track applications, views and clicks in your dashboard.

What the dashboard gives you

  • Vacancies with status badges: Draft, In review, Needs changes, Active, Rejected, Expired, Closed
  • Views, clicks and applications per vacancy, days left
  • Applications: CVs, notes, statuses (New → Seen → Shortlisted / Rejected)
  • A company profile and a “Verified company” badge given by the admin
  • Edit, close, renew, duplicate — in one click

Posting is free. To promote a vacancy (sponsored post, category sponsorship), see Advertising and partnership.

🛡 JobRadar never charges candidates. Vacancies that ask candidates for money, network marketing and suspicious posts are not published.

Frequently asked questions

Is posting really free?

Yes. Posting vacancies, receiving applications and the dashboard are free. Only optional promotion is paid.

When is my vacancy published?

After you submit it, it is “In review” and usually reviewed within 24 hours. Once approved it is published on the site and sent to matching candidates.

How long does a vacancy stay live?

30 days by default, up to 60 if you choose. When it ends the status is “Expired” and you can renew it in one click.

Why could a vacancy be rejected?

Most often: asking candidates for money, network marketing, several positions in one post, contact details inside the text, discrimination (age, gender), unrealistic salary, a duplicate. We always write the reason.

What if you ask for changes?

The dashboard shows “Needs changes” with what to fix. Press “Edit”, change it and submit again.

How do I close a vacancy?

Press “Close” in the dashboard: it leaves the site and is no longer sent. You can duplicate it later.

Where do I see applications?

In “Applications”: each candidate's CV, message and status. You are notified about new ones.

How is my data protected?

The contact person and private phone/e-mail are for moderation only and never published. Candidates' data is seen only by the employer they applied to. More: privacy policy.
