    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    What is JobRadar AZ?
    A service for people looking for work in Azerbaijan. It gathers vacancies from more than 30 websites and Telegram groups in one place and sends you the ones that fit on Telegram.
    Is JobRadar AZ free?
    Yes, starting is free: every day you get the 4 best-matching vacancies. If you want, the search pass sends up to 30 a day as soon as they are found: 7 days — 50 ⭐, 30 days — 150 ⭐ (Telegram Stars). The pass does not renew by itself.
    How do I find a job in Baku?
    Open @JobRadarAzBot on Telegram and choose your field: new vacancies in Baku will reach you every day. On the website you can also pick “Baku” as the city and see every post.
    I have no experience. Is there work for me?
    Yes. Choose the “For students” filter on the website: internships, part-time jobs and vacancies that need no experience are gathered there.
    Where do the vacancies come from?
    From job sites such as boss.az, jobsearch.az, hellojob.az, vezife.az and tap.az, from the career pages of banks and big companies, and from Telegram job groups: more than 30 websites in all.
    How do I apply for a vacancy?
    On the vacancy's page press “View the post / apply”. The post's own source opens and you apply directly to the employer. Applying is free.
    Is my personal data safe?
    We keep only what the service needs and never sell it. What we keep and why is written on the Privacy page.
    How do I avoid fake job posts?
    Don't answer posts that ask for money to hire you — for “documents”, “training” or a “uniform”. Check the company's name, and don't send a photo of your ID or your card details in the first message.
    How often do new vacancies come?
    We gather new vacancies several times a day. On the free plan the bot sends the best-matching ones twice a day, at 11:00 and 18:00 (Baku time); on Pro, as soon as they are found.
