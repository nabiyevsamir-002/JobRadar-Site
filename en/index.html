      Frequently asked questions

      Is JobRadar AZ free?
      Yes, completely free. You choose your field and the bot sends you new vacancies that fit, on Telegram — no payment at all.
      How does it work?
      Open @JobRadarAzBot on Telegram, type /start, choose your language and your field. After that, suitable posts arrive automatically.
      Where do the vacancies come from?
      From Azerbaijan's leading job sites (jobsearch.az, hellojob.az, tap.az, lalafo.az) and many Telegram job groups — we gather them all in one place.
      Is my personal data safe?
      We keep only the field you chose, to send you suitable posts. We never sell or share your personal data.
      How often do new posts come?
      We refresh the vacancies regularly during the day; as soon as a new one fits you, we let you know on Telegram.
